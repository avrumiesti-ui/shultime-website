Last Updated: February 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how ShulTime ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, stores, and shares your information when you use the ShulTime mobile application and related services (the "App").

This policy is designed to comply with applicable privacy laws and platform requirements, including Apple App Store and Google Play policies.

By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

a. Information You Provide

  • Account information (name, email address, login credentials)
  • Profile information (optional preferences, settings)

b. Automatically Collected Information

  • Device type, operating system, and app version
  • Usage data and interaction logs

c. Location Data

ShulTime may collect location information to provide relevant synagogue listings, prayer times, and nearby services. You can disable location access in your device settings.

We use your information to:

  • Provide customer support

We do not sell personal data.

3. Legal Bases for Processing (Where Applicable)

Depending on your jurisdiction, we rely on:

  • Legal compliance obligations

4. Data Retention

We retain personal data only as long as necessary to provide services and fulfill legal obligations.

Typical retention practices:

  • Account data: retained while your account is active

When no longer required, data is securely deleted or anonymized.

5. Account Deletion

You may request deletion of your account and personal data at any time through the App contact page or the website contact page.

Upon verified request:
